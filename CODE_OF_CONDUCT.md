# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique behavior and evidence, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation proportionately. Report incidents privately to a repository maintainer through their published contact channel. Reports will be handled with care and shared only as necessary to resolve the incident. Maintainers follow the same rules and must disclose conflicts of interest.
